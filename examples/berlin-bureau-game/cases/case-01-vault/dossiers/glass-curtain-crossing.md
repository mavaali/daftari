## Key Points

- The GLASS CURTAIN crossing network is a series of surveilled and counter-surveilled transit points along the sector boundary in divided Berlin.
- The Bureau uses these routes to move assets, couriers, and materials across the boundary.
- The Directorate is assessed to have partial awareness of at least two crossing points; active counter-surveillance is in place.
- NIGHTINGALE provides periodic observation reports on Directorate activity near the crossings — see [[fr-009-nightingale-curtain-watch]].
- If AMBER's defection is genuine, exfiltration under operation [[amber-signal]] will use a GLASS CURTAIN route.
- If AMBER is a dangle, any activated crossing point is burned — the Directorate would have the route.
