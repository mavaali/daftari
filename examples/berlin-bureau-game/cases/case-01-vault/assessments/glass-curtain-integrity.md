## Assessment

Based on [[fr-009-nightingale-curtain-watch]] (NIGHTINGALE, B3, 1971-03-28) and
[[fr-022-cartographer-amber-signal-route]] (CARTOGRAPHER, A2, 1971-04-16):

The GLASS CURTAIN network retains operational integrity with the following conditions:

- **ECHO crossing point:** suspended. NIGHTINGALE identified Directorate surveillance in
  static observation. Not to be activated until cleared.
- **FOXTROT crossing point:** assessed clear. NIGHTINGALE observed no Directorate activity;
  CARTOGRAPHER confirmed FOXTROT as the viable primary route for [[amber-signal]].
- **Overall network:** active and intact for FOXTROT. The Directorate has partial awareness of
  ECHO but has not yet interdicted or activated a counter-operation.

**Curation: evergreen.** Two independent Bureau sources — NIGHTINGALE (field observation) and
CARTOGRAPHER (operational assessment from a different vantage) — independently confirm FOXTROT
is clear. The Deputy Station Chief corroborated on 1971-04-17 after reviewing both reports.

**Caveat:** this assessment is conditional on AMBER not being a dangle. If [[amber-genuine-vs-dangle]]
resolves against AMBER, the Directorate may have directed the dangle specifically to provoke
FOXTROT activation — which would burn it. The integrity assessment holds pending that resolution.
