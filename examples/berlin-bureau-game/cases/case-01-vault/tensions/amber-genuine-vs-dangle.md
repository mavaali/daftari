## Tension

Two Bureau sources have filed directly contradictory reports on defector AMBER. One of them is wrong.

**Side A — AMBER is genuine** (NIGHTINGALE, [[fr-014-nightingale-amber-genuine]], B2, 1971-04-12):

NIGHTINGALE reports AMBER is a genuine defector. The approach was independent and unsolicited;
AMBER's described role and access are internally consistent; behavioral indicators during vetting
show no signs of a controlled approach. NIGHTINGALE's Admiralty code B2 reflects usually-reliable
sourcing and probably-true credibility, backed by a corroborating contact within NIGHTINGALE's
own network.

**Side B — AMBER is a dangle** (MAGPIE, [[fr-021-magpie-amber-dangle]], C3, 1971-04-15):

MAGPIE reports AMBER is a Directorate plant — a controlled false defector fed to the Bureau to
expose the GLASS CURTAIN crossing network. MAGPIE obtained this from a Directorate-adjacent contact.
MAGPIE's Admiralty code C3 reflects fairly-reliable sourcing and possibly-true credibility — lower
than NIGHTINGALE, but the operational stakes make the claim non-dismissible. A dangle designed to
burn [[glass-curtain]] is exactly the kind of operation the Directorate runs through a second-tier
courier precisely to avoid burning a higher-grade asset.

**Why this is factual, not interpretive:** both reports concern a verifiable fact — AMBER is either
a genuine defector or a Directorate plant. There is no "same facts, different interpretation" here.
One side must be wrong. The kind is `factual`.

**Temperature: 4 (hot).** The operation [[amber-signal]] is authorized in principle and the
exfiltration window is open. Activating on NIGHTINGALE alone (B2) and being wrong means burning
FOXTROT — the Bureau's cleanest remaining GLASS CURTAIN crossing — and exposing active assets.

**Stakes: DECISION.** Resolving this tension triggers a decision: execute [[amber-signal]] or abort.
The Station Chief will not execute without resolution.

## Bridge

Resolution paths available to the analyst:

1. **Find a third source** independently of NIGHTINGALE and MAGPIE who can corroborate or refute
   either claim. This is the cleanest path — it converts the working assessment to evergreen.
2. **Investigate MAGPIE's contact** — can the Directorate-adjacent source be identified and their
   access verified? If MAGPIE's sourcing cannot be established, the C3 grade may need to drop to D.
3. **Behavioral analysis of AMBER** — further vetting to identify probe-indicators consistent with
   a dangle operation. Requires direct Bureau contact with AMBER, which itself carries risk if AMBER
   is a plant.
4. **WEATHERVANE consideration** — if [[weathervane]] is confirmed as a Directorate double, the
   dangle scenario gains indirect corroboration (WEATHERVANE as the internal conduit). Does not
   resolve the tension but shifts the probability estimate.

Until one of these paths produces a determination, the tension stays open. The system is designed to
hold it here. A dangle hunt closed too early has historically cost more assets than any Directorate
operation.
