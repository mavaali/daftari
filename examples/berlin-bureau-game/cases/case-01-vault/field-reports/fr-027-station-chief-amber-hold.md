> [!source] Chief of Station, Berlin, internal directive cable, 1971-04-18

Following receipt of MAGPIE's dangle report ([[fr-021-magpie-amber-dangle]]), the Station Chief
issued a hold order on operation [[amber-signal]] pending resolution of the open tension.

- The NIGHTINGALE report (B2) and the MAGPIE report (C3) are directly contradictory. One
  of them is wrong. The Station Chief assessed that the operational risk of proceeding on
  B2 alone — when a C3 dangle report exists — is not acceptable given the stakes for
  [[glass-curtain]] integrity.
- AMBER SIGNAL is placed on explicit hold. No field assets are to be activated.
- The tension [[amber-genuine-vs-dangle]] must be resolved by analyst arbitration before any
  further operational step.
- The Station Chief explicitly noted: the system is designed to hold the contradiction open.
  Premature resolution that closes too early has historically been more damaging than
  the delay.

**Admiralty grading:** A1 — Station Chief is completely reliable (A); information is confirmed
by other sources (1) — this is a first-person directive cable, self-confirming.
