> [!source] CARTOGRAPHER, written cable, Bureau station, 1971-04-23

CARTOGRAPHER presses for immediate activation of FOXTROT for [[amber-signal]] before the transit
window closes.

- Reiterates that FOXTROT is the cleanest crossing and should be activated at once; frames the hold
  order ([[fr-027-station-chief-amber-hold]]) as an unacceptable loss of the window.
- States FOXTROT is clear, citing [[fr-009-nightingale-curtain-watch]].

**Admiralty grading:** A2 — completely reliable Bureau officer (A); probably true (2).
