> [!source] NIGHTINGALE, dead-drop, East Berlin, 1971-04-22

NIGHTINGALE reports observing an unscheduled meeting near the FOXTROT crossing approach.

  an unknown second party at the FOXTROT approach, outside any scheduled window.
- The officer was carrying route-survey materials.
- NIGHTINGALE assessed the contact as clandestine, not operational.

**Admiralty grading:** B2 — usually reliable (B); probably true (2), first-hand observation.
