## Summary

AMBER SIGNAL is the planned exfiltration of defector cryptonym AMBER from East Berlin. The operation
is authorized in principle under directive [[amber-exfil-go]] but execution is suspended pending
resolution of the AMBER bona-fides tension.

Case officer: CARTOGRAPHER — responsible for route selection and asset coordination. Planned
exfiltration route runs through a GLASS CURTAIN crossing point; the specific corridor is held
compartmented pending final go.

**Hold condition:** the tension [[amber-genuine-vs-dangle]] must be resolved before AMBER SIGNAL
activates. If AMBER is genuine (NIGHTINGALE, B2), proceed. If AMBER is a dangle (MAGPIE, C3),
abort — activating would burn the GLASS CURTAIN corridor and expose [[glass-curtain]] network assets.
