MAGPIE is a courier asset used for low-frequency, high-sensitivity document transit across the sector boundary.
Recruited via a third-party introduction; assessed source reliability C (fairly reliable) — useful but not
independently corroborated at the source level.

On 1971-04-15, MAGPIE submitted a debrief report asserting that defector AMBER is a Directorate dangle —
a controlled plant fed to the Bureau to expose the GLASS CURTAIN network. See [[fr-021-magpie-amber-dangle]].

This report directly contradicts NIGHTINGALE's assessment. The contradiction is filed as an open tension:
[[amber-genuine-vs-dangle]]. MAGPIE's reliability rating (C3 on the AMBER report) is lower than
NIGHTINGALE's (B2), but the claim cannot be dismissed — a dangle operation is exactly what the
Directorate would run through a second-tier courier to avoid burning a higher-grade asset.
