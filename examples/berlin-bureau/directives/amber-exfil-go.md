## Context

Following NIGHTINGALE's cable of 1971-04-12 (see [[fr-014-nightingale-amber-genuine]]), the Station
Chief reviewed the initial bona-fides assessment [[amber-bona-fides]] and authorized planning to
proceed for operation [[amber-signal]].

At the time of signing, only NIGHTINGALE's report had been received. The MAGPIE cable
([[fr-021-magpie-amber-dangle]]) had not yet been filed. This directive predates the open tension
[[amber-genuine-vs-dangle]] and does not constitute a final execute order — it authorizes planning
and route preparation only.

## Decision

**Go — planning authorized.** Operation AMBER SIGNAL may proceed to route-selection and asset-
staging phase. Execution requires a second, explicit go from the Station Chief after bona-fides
are confirmed. Any new contradicting intelligence must be surfaced before the execute order is
issued.

Case officer CARTOGRAPHER is authorized to begin GLASS CURTAIN route preparation under this
directive. No assets are to be activated in the field until the execute order.
